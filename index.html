Open T fleet platform

Internal transport management for a trucking company: vehicles, drivers, dispatch, fuel, inspections, maintenance and reporting, as one API with two clients.

  • API: Node and Express on MongoDB, 24 route groups, Socket.IO live tracking with a REST polling fallback, scheduled reports and expiry reminders.
  • Admin console: Next.js 16 and React 19 with Leaflet maps and Recharts, covering dispatch, rigs, owner-operators, repairs and printable handover acts.
  • Driver app: Flutter with Riverpod, shifts, jobs, fuel entries, documents and notifications.
  • Citizen identity and vehicle registry lookups through Mongolia's DAN OAuth service, with phone OTP for privileged roles.

Fuel Tank Warner

Watches every vehicle on the company's GPS server and raises an alert when fuel disappears, a tracker is tampered with, or a truck idles, speeds or goes silent. It runs on one office PC with a double-click start.

  • Backfills seven days of history on first start, then only notifies on new events.
  • Per-tank calibration tables turn raw sensor readings into litres.
  • Writes every refuel and suspected drain to an Excel ledger with map links and a verdict column.
  • Fleet, map, alerts and report views pushed live over server-sent events.

Diploma thesis2026 · MUST SICT

SentimentAI

Collects public Facebook page posts and comments, scores them for sentiment, finds topics and predicts engagement, then writes AI reports in Mongolian. The thesis defence version.

  • Four swappable sentiment back ends: lexicon, transformer, a fine-tuned Mongolian BERT, and a LoRA-tuned LLM served through Ollama.
  • LDA topic modelling, trend detection and an engagement model in scikit-learn.
  • Next.js dashboard behind an auth proxy, with Gemini for reports and Q&A, and a demo snapshot when the API is unreachable.
  • CI runs pytest and ruff for the API, eslint, tsc, vitest and a full build for the front end.
